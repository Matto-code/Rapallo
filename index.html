Friday — Cinque Terre

Early start locked in · 7am departure

Getting there

Direct trains run roughly every 20–30 minutes through the morning. Journey time is 30–55 minutes depending on train type. Buy the Cinque Terre Train Card at Monterosso station on arrival — unlimited hop-on trains between villages for the day (€5–10/adult, kids 4–11 half price).

Timetables are only published 3–4 months ahead — confirm the exact 7am service on the Trenitalia app closer to the date.
  • 7:00

    Train to Monterosso

    Rapallo → Monterosso, arriving roughly 7:45–8:00am, ahead of the crowds

  • 7:45

    Monterosso al Mare

    The only village with a proper sandy beach — early dip if anyone's keen, coffee and pastry, wander the old town and Castello Aurora viewpoint

  • 10:00

    Vernazza

    4-minute train hop. The most photogenic village — harbor, colorful houses, climb to Doria Castle for the classic view

  • 11:30

    Manarola — lunch

    Casual seafood along the harbor, then photos at the rocks or a short walk up the vineyard path. Corniglia can be skipped — it sits high on a cliff with no direct sea access

  • 13:30

    Riomaggiore

    Colorful cliffside houses, gelato, souvenir browsing. If the Via dell'Amore coastal path to Manarola is open, it's the flattest and most scenic walk in Cinque Terre — worth checking on arrival

  • 16:00

    Head back

    Train toward Rapallo — may involve a change at Sestri Levante depending on the specific service

  • 17:00

    Back in Rapallo

    Time to freshen up before dinner

Comfortable shoes for the stairs in every village, swimsuits and towels for Monterosso/Riomaggiore, and sun protection — little shade in peak afternoon. If everyone's tired, it's easy to cut Riomaggiore and linger longer in Vernazza or Manarola instead.
