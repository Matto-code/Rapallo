Local sweets to take home

Pandolce genovese is the local sweet bread, sold in two styles: a soft one, and a dry, crunchy, biscuit-like one called Baciccia. You'll find both in every bakery in town.

Montallegro by cable car

Mimma suggests a few hours up at the Sanctuary of Montallegro. The lower station is about 3 minutes' walk behind the train station.

  1. Ride up around 11:00–11:30. The ride takes a few minutes.
  2. Visit the sanctuary and enjoy the view.
  3. Lunch on the panoramic terrace at Casa del Pellegrino ("Il Pellegrino"), behind the church.
  4. Ride back down and spend the afternoon in town.
The cable car can stop in bad weather, so check before you go. It also pauses from 13:00 to 14:30, so catch a car up before 13:00.

Day trips

Portofino

See Castello Brown, the lighthouse (amazing view) and the church of San Giorgio. Map

San Fruttuoso

An abbey in a cove just past Portofino, reached by ferry. Allow 3–4 hours to visit and swim, then take the ferry back to Portofino. Map

  • For swimmers and divers: the underwater Christ of the Abyss statue, on your own or with an organised boat trip.
  • For lunch, Mimma recommends Laura's restaurant. She's happy to help you book it.
Mimma's full-day route: boat from Rapallo straight to San Fruttuoso (2–3 hours there, longer with a swim or lunch), then boat to Portofino, explore, and walk back to Santa Margherita.

Cinque Terre

Go by train or tourist boat. Head straight to Riomaggiore, then work your way back through Manarola, Corniglia and Vernazza to Monterosso, the largest village and closest to Rapallo.

In high season, trains after 5 pm are less frequent and you could wait over an hour. Check your return times. Timetables: trenitalia.com

Ferry: Servizio Marittimo del Tigullio

From the timetable photo Mimma sent (2025 fares). Buy tickets in advance and check times, since the info point can get busy. Tickets must be bought the same day, at least 20 minutes before departure, and allow stops for the whole day. Times can change with weather and sea conditions.

Around town

  • Castello di Rapallo MapBuilt 1550–1551 on the Lungomare Vittorio Veneto to defend against pirates, after the corsair Dragut sacked the town in July 1549. Remarkably well preserved and the pride of Rapallo. Allow about an hour.
